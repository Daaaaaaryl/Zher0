## Future revision direction

The Sangu artwork is the canonical color/material reference for the equipment family.

Future Kegutsu revisions should match the approved Sangu palette and rendering:

- Charcoal / near-black armor
- Warm muted antique gold
- Warm brown/copper cords rather than bright red
- Warm ivory cloth where appropriate
- Restrained highlights
- Clean 2D anime/RPG rendering
- Simple Zero-to-Hero baseline styling

Do not recolor or otherwise transform the current approved Kegutsu master. This guidance applies only to a future revision.
