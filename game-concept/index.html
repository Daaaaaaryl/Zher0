Zero to Hero

Your power is not simply your level.

In Z'her0, equipment is not decoration. It is a major part of your identity. Change your equipment and you change what your character can do, how hard they hit, how well they defend, how fast they move, and eventually which moves are available to them.

The character is the person. The equipment determines what that person is capable of doing.

Combat system

When attacks meet, they do not simply pass through each other.

A Clash happens when two compatible attacks collide. Instead of both sides taking full damage, the attacks neutralize part of each other.

1

Both sides commit

Each character chooses a combat move.

2

A Clash can occur

The player can create Clash intent, while the system still decides whether the attacks successfully meet.

3

Attacks neutralize

The two attack values collide. Only the remaining force continues through the normal defense layer.

Impact

Speed changes how hard an attack lands into a Clash.

Being faster does not automatically make you win. SP influences Impact — a multiplier applied to the attack before the two Clash values are compared.

Power is what you bring. Impact is how effectively you deliver it.
Working example
100 PA × 1.10 Impact = 110 Clash Damage
120 PA × 0.90 Impact = 108

The weaker but faster attack can sometimes overcome a stronger but slower one — without making Speed an automatic win button.

Clash rhythm

Clashes should not happen forever.

Repeated successful Clashes make another immediate Clash less likely. A turn without a Clash allows that chance to recover. The exact percentages are still being balanced, but the behavior prevents endless equal-power collisions.

Equipment is part of combat

Your gear can change during the fight.

One idea under development is temporary equipment stress. Instead of permanently destroying gear, a failed use could temporarily reduce a stat that item contributes during the current battle.

Boots attempt a dodge → dodge fails → temporary SP loss

That creates tactical adaptation: if your speed drops, maybe you stop trying to dodge and switch to guarding or clashing. After battle, the equipment can return to normal.

Progression philosophy
You are not chasing a bigger number just because your level went up.
You are chasing access, options, equipment, combinations, and mastery.

Levels can open doors. Equipment changes who walks through them.
