## Art direction

- Equipment family: Knight set
- Baseline Zero-to-Hero, practical early-career medieval equipment
- Worn but maintained steel, natural brown leather, and dark navy/blue padded fabric where appropriate
- High-quality polished 2D RPG equipment illustration with restrained design
- Transparent background and a clean, readable inventory silhouette
- No pixel art, character model, embedded text, presentation frame, environment, or alternate views
- No heraldic crest, royal insignia, elaborate gold trim, glow, magic, or excessive fantasy ornamentation
